# Blinkit | Partner Strike & Compensation Analysis

## Overview  
This project analyzes delivery partner dissatisfaction at Blinkit using a business-first approach.  
The goal is to understand what is affecting partner experience and identify practical changes that can improve satisfaction without reducing operational coverage.

---

## Problem  
Blinkit is facing delivery partner strikes that are affecting operations and customer experience.
The company wants to understand the real reasons behind this issue using data.

NOTE: Because real internal data is not available, I am using hypothetical but realistic company-style data to simulate the problem.

---

## What I Did  

- Cleaned and prepared raw operational data in **Python**  
- Performed business analysis using **MySQL**  
- Built an executive-style dashboard in **Power BI**  
- Focused on partner experience, distance, earnings, complaints, and city/store-level impact  

---

## Key Findings  

- Long-distance deliveries earn **significantly less per kilometer** than short-distance deliveries  
- Partners in large cities like **Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore** travel more but are paid worse per km  
- Churn alone does not represent partner satisfaction  
- Customer complaints are not mainly about late delivery, indicating deeper operational and experience issues  

**Main Insight:**  
The compensation structure is broken. High-effort deliveries are rewarded the least.

---

## Business Recommendations  

- Redesign pay to scale fairly with delivery distance  
- Add incentives for long-distance orders  
- Apply city-level rate adjustments in high-load areas  
- Identify and fix high-stress stores that create excessive partner workload  

---

## Tools Used  

- **Python** – Data cleaning and feature engineering  
- **MySQL** – Business analysis and querying  
- **Power BI** – Executive dashboard and storytelling  

---

## Outcome  

This project shows how data can be used not just to report numbers, but to diagnose system-level problems and recommend practical business solutions focused on partner experience.
